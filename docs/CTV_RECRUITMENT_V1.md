# CTV Recruitment V1

`/ctv/join` is the selective recruitment and assessment route. It remains separate from the optional policy reference at `/chinh-sach/cong-tac-vien` and the approved-partner workspace at `/partner`.

The assessment observes distribution + judgement + communication + system fit, not MacBook memorization. Its seven stable question IDs are preserved in the immutable `mbmc.ctv-application-answers.v1` snapshot.

The `?experience=ctv-join` homepage context is session-tab orientation only. It does not authenticate, identify, establish referral provenance, or modify recommendation and customer behavior.

`ctv_applications` in Care is canonical. Telegram is a best-effort interrupt. Owner review is read-only and V1 has no automated scoring or admission.

CTV applicant ≠ CTV partner ≠ staff.
